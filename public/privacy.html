Privacy

Fine Print reads public market data and stores public facts about public markets.

On this page
  1. What we keep
  2. What we send

§ 1What we keep

  • No accounts, emails or tracking cookies. Visitors are not tracked. The theme you pick is kept in your own browser.
  • Precedents and rulings describe public Polymarket markets. They are stored encrypted on Walrus, and the blob IDs are public.
  • Paid rulings record the paying Sui address and the transaction digest, both already public on chain.
Your theme choice is stored in your browser under the key fp-theme. Nothing else is.

§ 2What we send

  • Market text is sent to the judge model's API provider to produce a ruling.
