Price board

Price follows the record

Each wording pattern has its own price. A pattern the court calls well costs more. One it keeps missing gets cheaper.

accuracy = (correct + 1) / (total + 2), so 50% with no history.
price = base × (0.5 + accuracy), kept between 0.01 and 0.05 USDC.

When the court was wrong

A paid LOW ruling followed by a dispute, or by the outcome the crowd priced as less likely, gets its fee back in USDC.

For bots

Ask, pay, get a cited ruling

No account and no API key. The 402 answer tells your bot what to pay.

  1. 1
    Call GET /api/ruling?market=<id>
  2. 2
    Get 402 with the price and the Sui address to pay.
  3. 3
    Sign a USDC payment on Sui mainnet and retry with the X-PAYMENT header.
  4. 4
    The server checks the digest on chain, then returns the ruling with its precedents.
