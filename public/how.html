How it works

One engine, three ways in: the trader view, a paid court endpoint for bots, and a record of rule versions.

§ 1Precedents live in Walrus Memory

Every resolved Polymarket market we read becomes one memory: its key rule clause, its resolution source, the crowd price before the ruling, the UMA status sequence and the outcome. Memories are written to a MemWal account on Sui mainnet and stored as encrypted blobs on Walrus. Each one has a blob ID you can open on Walruscan.

Every blob ID on this site opens on Walruscan. The latest ones are listed on Proof.

§ 2A ruling cites its precedents

When you open a market, Fine Print fetches its rules and price history through Tatum's Prediction Market API, recalls the closest precedents and learned wording patterns, and asks a judge model for a verdict: HIGH, MED or LOW dispute risk. The judge must cite the blob IDs it relied on. A ruling without citations is refused.

Market text is passed in as untrusted data, so instructions hidden in a description are ignored.

Rules and prices come from Tatum first. If Tatum has no data, Polymarket Gamma and CLOB are the fallback, and the chart says which one it used.

§ 3Rulings are sealed before the outcome

Every ruling is written to memory before the market resolves. When it resolves, the ruling is graded, the grade is written next to it, and the wording pattern's track record moves. The price of a ruling for that pattern follows its accuracy.

Grades are written to the court's own memory account, next to the ruling they grade.

§ 4Bots pay per ruling, and get refunds

GET /api/ruling?market=<id> answers HTTP 402. A bot pays in USDC on Sui testnet using the x402 protocol (memory is on mainnet; payments run on testnet for this entry). The server settles and checks the payment through Tatum's Sui gateway before it answers. If a LOW ruling is followed by a dispute, the fee goes back to the payer in USDC, and the refund digest is public on the court page.

§ 5Rule versions

Rule text is hashed and saved as a new version whenever it changes. Historical clarifications are read from the UMA adapter's bulletin board on Polygon through Tatum's Polygon RPC. On the chart, each version is a grey pin.

On the chart: grey pin = a rule version. "You bought under v1" means the rules changed after you bought.

Replays use only precedents whose market closed before the replay time.
